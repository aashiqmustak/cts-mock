HEALTHCARE PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
DOCUMENTED RULES — MULTI-SERVICE STARTER SET

IMPORTANT
There is no single PDF containing the rules for all medical services. CMS coverage rules are distributed across National Coverage Determinations (NCDs), Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs), and Coverage/Billing Articles.

This document is a starter rulebook for a prototype Prior Authorization Rules Engine. The rules are Medicare/CMS-specific, and some LCDs are jurisdiction-specific. They must not be treated as universal private-insurer rules or clinical advice.

==================================================
1. ROLE OF THE THREE DATA SOURCES
==================================================

CMS Provider Utilization Data
- Provider, service, procedure and utilization features.
- Useful for realistic provider/procedure patterns.

MEPS
- Healthcare utilization, expenditure, visits, medicines and source-of-payment variables.
- Useful as contextual features.

FDA / DailyMed
- Official drug-label information such as indications, dosage, warnings and contraindications.
- Mainly useful when the request involves medication.

CMS NCD / LCD Policy Documents
- Actual source for MRI and other medical-service coverage/medical-necessity and documentation rules.
- These are separate from the CMS provider utilization dataset.

2. MRI
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS MRI NCD/NCA
- CMS LCD L34220 — Lumbar MRI
- CMS LCD L34425 — Magnetic Resonance Imaging of the Head and Neck

MRI-01 — Covered indication / medical necessity
Rule:
The requested MRI must have a covered indication and be reasonable and necessary for the specific patient under the applicable CMS policy.
Extract:
Diagnosis, clinical indication, reason for MRI.

MRI-02 — Supporting medical record
Rule:
Clinical documentation supporting the condition and treatment must be maintained and available for review when required.
Extract:
Doctor/clinical note and relevant treatment history.

MRI-03 — Diagnosis support
Rule:
The medical record should support the diagnosis/ICD-10-CM code reported for the MRI service.
Extract:
Diagnosis, ICD-10-CM code and clinical rationale.

MRI-04 — Physician order
Rule:
For the cited Lumbar MRI LCD, an attending/treating physician order is required for each MRI test.
Extract:
Signed and dated physician order.

MRI-05 — Clinical rationale
Rule:
An imaging abnormality by itself is not enough to establish necessity; the record should contain additional clinical rationale.
Extract:
Symptoms, findings, history and reason for imaging.

MRI-06 — Lumbar MRI documentation
Rule:
For the cited Lumbar MRI policy, documentation should clearly support why the MRI is necessary and reasonable.
Extract:
Clinical rationale and relevant findings.

MRI-07 — Head/neck MRI documentation
Rule:
For the cited Head and Neck MRI policy, documentation supporting medical necessity must be legible, maintained in the medical record and available upon request.
Extract:
Legible medical record and supporting documentation.

MRI-08 — Additional same-day imaging
Rule:
For the cited Head and Neck MRI policy, when MRI and CT/myelography are performed for the same condition on the same day, documentation should support the necessity for the additional evaluation.
Extract:
Explanation for additional same-day imaging.

MRI-09 — Patient-specific MRI considerations
Rule:
Check patient-specific device/contraindication circumstances against the applicable current MRI policy. Do not use a blanket rule without checking the policy.
Extract:
Implanted device information and relevant documentation.

MRI-10 — Human review for ambiguity
Rule:
If required information is missing, conflicting or ambiguous, route the case to human review instead of inventing clinical facts.
Extract:
Missing/ambiguous information flag.

3. CT / CARDIAC CT / CCTA
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS NCD §220.1
- CMS CCTA LCD/Articles

CT-01 — Physician order
Rule:
Tests must be ordered by the physician or other qualified practitioner treating the beneficiary in the applicable policy.

CT-02 — Medical necessity
Rule:
The record must support the medical necessity of the diagnostic test.

CT-03 — Clinical evidence
Rule:
Relevant history, physical examination and pertinent test/procedure results should support the request.

CT-04 — Diagnosis/coding
Rule:
ICD-10-CM codes should reflect the patient's condition and reason for the service.

CT-05 — Report/documentation
Rule:
A formal written report and relevant imaging/reconstruction documentation should be maintained for the cited CCT/CCTA policy.

==================================================
4. PET SCAN
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS PET Scan coverage/Billing Article A59318

PET-01 — Medical necessity
Rule:
The record must fully support medical necessity under the applicable policy.

PET-02 — Clinical history
Rule:
Relevant medical history and physical examination should be documented.

PET-03 — Supporting tests
Rule:
Pertinent diagnostic test/procedure and imaging results should be available.

PET-04 — Clinical rationale
Rule:
The record should explain why PET is needed and, where applicable, why PET is preferred over standard imaging.

PET-05 — Formal report
Rule:
A formal written report identifying the patient, reason for the test, interpretation and relevant images should be maintained.

5. ECHOCARDIOGRAPHY
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS LCD L37379
- CMS LCD L34338

ECHO-01 — Medical necessity
Rule:
The record must fully support medical necessity under the applicable echocardiography policy.

ECHO-02 — Clinical indication
Rule:
The requested echo should match an indication covered by the applicable policy.

ECHO-03 — Ordering
Rule:
Diagnostic tests must be ordered by the treating physician who uses the result for management of the specific medical problem.

ECHO-04 — Clinical evidence
Rule:
Relevant history, physical examination and pertinent diagnostic test results should support the service.

ECHO-05 — Formal report
Rule:
A formal written report with interpretation and appropriate measurements/image documentation should be maintained.

==================================================
6. SLEEP STUDY / POLYSOMNOGRAPHY
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS LCD L36839/L36861
- CMS NCD §240.4.1

SLEEP-01 — Medical evidence
Rule:
Documentation must support the need for diagnostic testing, such as signs, symptoms, complaints, examination or laboratory evidence.

SLEEP-02 — Order/referral
Rule:
The sleep clinic should maintain the attending physician's order/referral.

SLEEP-03 — Covered condition
Rule:
Diagnostic testing is covered only when the applicable medical-condition criteria are met.

SLEEP-04 — Duplicate testing
Rule:
Duplicative testing is not reasonable and necessary when prior results remain pertinent.

SLEEP-05 — Home sleep test
Rule:
The cited policy requires a comprehensive sleep evaluation and high pretest probability of moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea for HST; device/training documentation requirements also apply.

7. OUTPATIENT PHYSICAL THERAPY
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS LCD L34428
- CMS therapy guidance

PT-01 — Medical necessity
Rule:
Documentation must support that therapy is medically necessary and that covered services were performed.

PT-02 — Dates/services
Rule:
Records should show what service was performed and on which dates.

PT-03 — Evaluation and plan of care
Rule:
Required documentation includes evaluation and plan of care plus pertinent patient characteristics.

PT-04 — Clinical history
Rule:
History/physical examination and response or changes from previously administered skilled services should be documented.

PT-05 — Skilled-service rationale
Rule:
A detailed rationale should explain why skilled services are needed in light of the patient's overall condition.

8. COLONOSCOPY
==================================================

Policy source:
- CMS Diagnostic Colonoscopy LCDs/Articles

COLON-01 — Medical necessity
Rule:
Ordering/referring physician documentation must indicate the medical necessity of the colonoscopy.

COLON-02 — Diagnosis
Rule:
The medical record should support the ICD-10-CM diagnosis reported.

COLON-03 — Order
Rule:
Where applicable, the ordering/referring physician's order should be maintained.

COLON-04 — Procedure report
Rule:
The colonoscopy report should be maintained; applicable policies require details such as extent/depth and abnormal findings.

COLON-05 — Pathology
Rule:
When applicable, associated pathology reports should be maintained in the medical record.

9. GENERAL DIAGNOSTIC-SERVICE RULES
==================================================

GENERAL-01 — Reasonable and necessary
Rule:
Medicare coverage generally requires services to be reasonable and necessary for diagnosis or treatment of illness/injury under the applicable benefit and policy.

GENERAL-02 — Documentation
Rule:
Documentation must support the service and be available for review when requested.

GENERAL-03 — Coding
Rule:
Diagnosis/procedure coding should accurately describe the patient's condition and service.

GENERAL-04 — Human review
Rule:
Ambiguous, incomplete or conflicting cases should be routed to qualified human review.

GENERAL-05 — Policy scope
Rule:
Always apply the exact current NCD/LCD/Article and jurisdiction. Do not assume a rule applies to every payer.

10. HOW TO USE THESE RULES IN YOUR AI SYSTEM
==================================================

Step 1:
Identify the requested service: MRI, CT, PET, Echocardiography, Sleep Study, Physical Therapy, Colonoscopy, etc.

Step 2:
Retrieve the applicable current CMS policy for that service and jurisdiction.

Step 3:
Upload medical report(s) and use OCR/PDF extraction if needed.

Step 4:
Extract only required fields:
- Diagnosis
- Symptoms
- Clinical findings
- Previous treatment
- Physician order/referral
- Supporting tests
- Service-specific documents

Step 5:
Evaluate the documented rules and record each rule as:
- SATISFIED
- NOT_SATISFIED
- UNKNOWN

Step 6:
If required information is missing or conflicting, use HUMAN_REVIEW rather than guessing.

Step 7:
Pass validated structured features to the ML model.

Step 8:
Combine ML prediction with mandatory policy rules. The ML model must not silently override a mandatory policy criterion.

Step 9:
Store the rule source, extracted evidence, ML version, prediction and final recommendation in the audit log.

==================================================
FINAL LIMITATION
==================================================

This is a prototype starter ruleset, not an exhaustive database of all healthcare services.

CMS NCDs are national Medicare policies. LCDs can be jurisdiction-specific. Policies change over time. For a real implementation, always retrieve and version the current applicable policy.

The ML model should support qualified human review and should not be presented as independently making clinical decisions.
